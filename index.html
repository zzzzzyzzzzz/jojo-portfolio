SEA&WIN brought the World Cup beyond the stadium. Fans walk themed tours across Seattle and Washington State, check in at stops, answer quizzes and play AR soccer mini-games to earn points, then redeem them for prizes at local partner sites.

The free app launched in March 2026 and ran through the tournament as part of SeattleFWC26’s legacy work, sending visitors to public art, cultural spaces and small businesses across the state.

About

I design for the moments when nobody has time to read: a fighter mid-exchange, a fan on a street corner with one bar of signal, a transaction that settles before you blink.

I came to design sideways. Before this I spent years writing code and working as a technical artist, so I sketch in Figma and in the engine, and I care about how a thing gets built as much as how it looks.

Open to new roles. If you’re making something people use on the move, I’d love to hear about it.
